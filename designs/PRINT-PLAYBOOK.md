# Bigben print playbook

What the 39 tech-pack pages (NDA Apparels, Jan–Sep 2026) show about how Bigben and its sister label mmunna build print catalogues. Use this alongside `SEGMENTS.md` for every new catalogue.

## 1. Sizes, segments and style codes

| Size | Segment | Ages | Style suffix | Sample size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 16, 18, 20 | Kids | 1–3 | **K** | 20 (sometimes 18) |
| 22, 24, 26, 28 | Juniors | 3–10 | **J** | 28 |
| 30, 32, 34, 36 | Boys | 10–14 | **B** | 34 |

Mixed catalogues split the run into two size sets, A and B:
- **K/J:** A 18-20-22, B 24-26-28
- **J/B:** A 22-24-26(-28), B 28/30-32-34(-36)
- Some J/B packs run A 20-22-24, B 26-28-30

A mixed catalogue's prints must work at both ends of its age span.

## 2. How a catalogue is built

- **Colourways:** 3–8 per style, most often 6 or 8. Each colourway carries its **own print**; prints don't repeat inside a catalogue.
  - Exceptions are deliberate. *Pairs*: two colours share one print (0936 J/B, 0941 J/B co-ords). *Single print*: one print across 3 colours (0932 K/J).
- **Distinct, not similar:** no two prints in a catalogue use the same idea, layout or technique. A typical boys catalogue (e.g. 0821 B) mixes:
  - an icon (headphones)
  - bold letters (OB)
  - an object (coffee cup, soda can)
  - script wordmarks (Brotherhood, Street Generation)
  - a single initial (A)
  - graffiti type (GAMER)
- **One theme can still vary:** 0943 K/J is all dinosaurs, but every print uses a different treatment:
  - a row of colourful dinos at the hem
  - a skeleton with speed lines
  - "Jurassic Dino Saur World" type
  - a poster-style T-rex
  - giant cropped "TRACK" type
  - a comic-panel dino
- **Garment palette:** mostly muted and earthy, rarely bright. Common colours: fawn, khaki, mouse, coffee, rust, mehandi, pista, onion, mint, sand, bottle green, CK blue, cement, plus black and navy.

## 3. Print size and placement by silhouette

| Silhouette | Front | Back | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R/N H/S, Kids (K)** | One **large** front print (8–10"), often off-centre or a placement print: wraps the body, sits at the hem, or crops off the edge | Usually none | Trompe-l'oeil ideas: printed backpack straps on the front with the backpack on the back (0822 K) |
| **R/N H/S, Boys (B)** | **Tiny** chest mark (≈3", logo size) | Medium upper-back print (≈8–10") | 0821 B: front is almost blank, the story is on the back |
| **R/N H/S, J/B jersey** | Mid-chest number + crests (varsity/motorsport: 23, 45, 77, 98) | – | Sleeve prints + **studs** on shoulders (0960 J/B) |
| **R/N F/S, Kids** | Small chest mark (≈3–4") | Large back (≈9–10") | Vehicles, pixel dino, surf van (0903 K) |
| **R/N F/S, Junior / Boys** | Small chest mark | Large back (≈10–12") | Front-only variants use one large centre print (0942 J/B) |
| **Baggy F/S / Tencel digital** | Full-front **photo-real DTF** print, edge to edge, sleeve hits | – | 0930 K/J, 0955 J/B |
| **Collar H/S (polo), Kids** | **Big placement prints**: low at the hem, side-stacked, wrapping from body onto sleeve; suspenders with "toy charm" prints | – | Contrast sleeves, sleeve stripes (0804 K, 0853 K) |
| **Collar H/S, Junior / Boys** | Small chest badge (≈3") | Medium-large back (≈9–11") | Tipped collars, sleeve text; canvas polos add asymmetric side prints, vertical text on the placket |
| **Fancy collar H/S, Boys** | **Engineered border prints** down the placket and around the hem (resort / heritage motifs) | – | 0938 B: pagoda, palms, baroque, florals |
| **Drop shoulder / oversized** | Small chest wordmark (≈4–5") or small left-chest icon | **Largest prints**: 12–14" wide, from below the collar to mid-back | Streetwear: bubble/puff graffiti type, drip letters, collages, racing graphics (0836 B, 0838 B, 0832 B) |
| **Drop shoulder, city themes** | Mid-chest print + floral + photo frames | Vertical photo strip + text | Black-and-white photo collage: Tokyo, London, Paris, Roma, Berlin (0963 J/B) |
| **Sets: tee + bermuda / co-ord** | Tee front large (≈8–10"); sometimes a small back | – | **Matching small leg hit** on the bermuda or jogger that echoes the tee, or vertical text down the leg (0791 J, 0831 K/J, 0941 J/B) |

Rule of thumb:
- **Oversized / drop shoulder:** the biggest back print.
- **Crew neck (boys):** the smallest chest print.
- **Kids:** one big, playful front print.

## 4. Print types and when to use them

- **Wording / typography:** varsity arches, script signatures, bubble/puff graffiti, drip letters, cropped giant type, stacked slogans ("Never give up", "Stick to your guns", "All good", "Let the vibes flow").
  - Boys and drop-shoulder catalogues lean heavily on this.
- **Cartoon:** this does **not** always mean a character with eyes, hands and legs. It can be:
  - an object that looks like a **toy**: toy truck, excavator, school bus, jeep, aeroplanes, a model-kit sprue of race-car parts (0858 K), surfboards, headphones, a game controller, an arcade cabinet
  - a **character**: animal, food with sunglasses (banana), robot
  - Kids = toys and soft characters; Junior = cooler characters and gaming; Boys = streetwear characters (bears, designer toys) used sparingly.
- **Simple / minimal:** single initial, small icon + tiny wordmark, line-art. Mostly boys' chest prints and boys' polos.
- **Photo print (DTF / digital):** photo-real objects (jeep, sneaker, sports car, sunny-side egg), city photo collages, photo-real 3D characters (rabbit, teddy, skater), and textures (fuzzy lion mane, denim). Often mixed with flat graphics in one print.
- **Embellishment:** puff print, stickers/patches, studs, embroidery/appliqué, sleeve prints, shoulder piping, contrast tipping.

## 5. Kids vs Junior vs Boys at a glance

- **Kids (K):** vehicles, animals, dinos, food with faces, sunny/happy words; big friendly front prints; playful placement and trompe-l'oeil.
- **Juniors (J):** gaming, space, adventure/explore, cooler characters, slogans; chest + back; still colourful.
- **Boys (B):** streetwear: graffiti/bubble type, script, varsity/racing numbers, collage, minimal chest marks, large backs on drop shoulders; muted palette.

## 6. Rights watch-list

Several tech packs use protected names or characters. These are a legal risk on garments sold commercially and must not be repeated in new designs:
- **Anime characters:** Naruto / Jujutsu Kaisen (0955 J/B)
- **Games:** Sonic, Minecraft (0856 J/B, 0862 B); Astro Bot and the PlayStation logo (0858 K)
- **Fashion brands:** Stüssy logo (0859 J); "Balenciaga" parody (0821 B)
- **Smiley faces:** designs close to the Smiley Company's smiley (0838 B, 0854 J, 0903 K)
