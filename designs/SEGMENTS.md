# Bigben design segments (from the founder)

Every print brief names a gender and one or more age segments. Design for exactly those segments. See `PRINT-PLAYBOOK.md` for print size, placement and catalogue structure taken from the founder's tech packs.

## Age segments

| Segment | Ages | Boys | Girls |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kids | 1–3 | Kids (boys) | Kids (girls) |
| Juniors | 3–10 | Juniors (boys) | Juniors (girls) |
| Boys / Senior girls | 10–14 | Boys | Senior girls |

Catalogues can mix segments, for example "Junior + Boys" (3–14) or "Kids + Juniors" (1–10). Mixed catalogues need designs that work across the whole age span.

## Direction per segment

- **Kids (1–3):** soft, cute, simple characters and big friendly shapes. Founder's references: *Dino Dept.* and *Cool Kid* (Sticker Club).
- **Juniors (3–10):** playful cartoon characters, space, gaming, adventure. Founder's references: *Space Club*, *Play*, *Game Night*.
- **Boys (10–14):** more premium and minimal; type-led, streetwear, understated. Founder's reference: *Out Of Office*.
- **Boys vs girls:** boys' catalogues get boyish designs and girls' catalogues get girlish ones. Florals, daisies and similar motifs (e.g. *Sunday Club*) are for girls' ranges.

## Founder feedback so far

- Liked: the overall direction of collections C1–C6.
- Too old-school, avoid this look: *Night Riders* (bicycle), *Sun Club / Sunshine Society*.
- Look at current Indian high-street kidswear (Reliance Trends, Max, etc.) for trend direction.
- Never use licensed characters (Marvel, Disney, anime, etc.).
